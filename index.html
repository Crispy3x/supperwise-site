Supperwise

A week of family dinners, planned under your grocery budget and priced by the pack you actually buy.

Every dinner works for everyone at the table: diets, allergies and spice levels, person by person. Nearly 300 recipes, one grocery list with a total, school lunches, first foods for babies, and a food scan that scores any meal from 1 to 100.

Your first week is free to keep. Coming soon to the App Store for iPhone.
